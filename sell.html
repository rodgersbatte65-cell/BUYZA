🏍️ Boda operator? Post your ride service

Follow these steps. Your ride listing will appear with other BUYZA services.

  1. Finish your Boda operator registration and have an active Standard or Premium ride plan.
  2. Choose Boda Boda ride service below.
  3. Add your service area, phone number, starting fare, and a short description.
  4. Tap Post my Boda service. Passengers can then book from your listing.
Check or activate my ride plan
Your ride plan will be checked when you choose the Boda option.
What are you posting?
For a service, choose Services. For a Boda ride, use the Boda option above.
Photos are optional for services. For products, choose 1 to 5 photos. The first photo is the main listing image. Each photo must be 5 MB or smaller.
